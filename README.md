**Sirius** is a lightweight, zero-jargon financial co-pilot built specifically for independent retail owners, bakeries, cafés, and boutique shops. It bridges the gap between bank accounts, point-of-sale (POS) systems, and distributor payables—predicting cash crunches before they happen and recommending instant, one-click solutions to free up trapped capital.

---

## 📌 The Problem & Solution

* **The Problem:** 82% of small retail businesses fail due to cash flow mismatches and trapped inventory, not a lack of customer demand. Business owners spend hours in messy spreadsheets, frequently blindsided by payroll shortfalls after over-purchasing stock.
* **The Sirius Solution:** A proactive decision engine that translates complex balance sheets into a simple **Stoplight Health System** (Green/Yellow/Red) with actionable 1-click remedies (e.g., delaying non-critical supplier invoices or flash-discounting dead inventory).

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🟢 **Stoplight Financial Status:** Real-time visibility into your cash safety zone (Safe, Caution, Urgent Action) with plain-English summaries.
* 📈 **Interactive Scenario Simulator:** Live "What-If?" forecasting (30 / 60 / 90 days) showing how invoice deferrals or promotions impact runway.
* ⚡ **1-Click Action Cards:** Instant recommendations to protect liquidity without manual calculations.
* 🛒 **"Can I Afford It?" Purchase Checker:** Quick-calculator widget checking planned expenses against upcoming payroll and rent obligations.
* 📱 **Weekly Digest Engine:** Preview automated weekly WhatsApp/SMS summaries designed for store owners on the move.
